DataStage HA Pre-Production Checklist

  • N+1 worker capacity provisioned and verified under peak load
  • Standby conductor configured and health-checked
  • Conductor failover (auto + manual) tested and RTO measured
  • Repository database deployed with HA (HADR / Patroni / Multi-AZ)
  • Daily metadata export scheduled and off-host storage verified
  • All job assets on shared HA filesystem — no local-only paths
  • Long-running jobs designed with restart / checkpoint capability
  • Client VIP or load balancer configured and tested post-failover
  • Service ports bound to specific IPs — not 0.0.0.0
  • Monitoring alerts configured for conductor, workers, repo DB, filesystem
  • Worker failure scenario tested (job abort + restart)
  • Full DR runbook documented and approved by platform team
