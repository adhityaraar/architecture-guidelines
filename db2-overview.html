Db2 HADR — Overview

IBM Db2 High Availability Disaster Recovery (HADR)

IBM Db2 HADR replicates log records from a primary to one or more standby databases over TCP. The standby continuously replays logs; on primary failure it is promoted (takeover) to become the new primary.

Goals & Targets

GoalTarget
RPO (SYNC mode)Zero
RPO (NEARSYNC mode)< 2 seconds
RTO (automatic takeover)< 30 seconds
Availability99.99%
Maximum standbys3 (Db2 11.1+)

Core Concepts

TermDefinition
Primary DatabaseActive instance accepting read/write workloads; ships logs to standby(s).
Standby DatabaseContinuously updated replica; can take over the primary role.
Log ShippingRedo log records transmitted from primary to standby over TCP.
TakeoverPromotion of standby to primary — manual, by force, or automatic.
Log GapDifference between primary and standby log sequence number (LSN).
ACRAutomatic Client Reroute — redirects clients after takeover.
Reads on StandbyRead-only SQL directed to standby to offload primary.
