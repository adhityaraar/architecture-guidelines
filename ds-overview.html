DataStage HA — Overview

High availability design for IBM DataStage parallel engine, conductor/worker resilience, and repository HA.

IBM DataStage HA spans three distinct layers: the conductor/worker parallel engine, the repository and metadata store, and the job scheduler and project services.

Goals & Targets

GoalTarget
Job restart on worker failureAutomatic (within current job run)
Service recovery (conductor)< 5 minutes (Tier 2)
Repository RPO0 s (synchronous DB replication)
Metadata / project RPO< 60 s
Scheduled job continuityResume from last checkpoint after recovery

Architecture Components

ComponentRoleHA Strategy
Engine Tier (conductor)Orchestrates parallel job executionN+1 conductor nodes; auto-failover via cluster manager
Worker NodesExecute parallel job partitionsN+1 worker pool; failed workers trigger job restart
Repository DBStores job designs, metadata, logsHADR (Db2) or streaming replication (PostgreSQL)
