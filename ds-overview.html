DataStage HA — Overview

High availability design for IBM DataStage parallel engine, conductor/worker resilience, and repository HA.

IBM DataStage (DataStage as a Service / CP4D / standalone) is an ETL/ELT platform whose HA design spans three distinct layers: the conductor/worker parallel engine, the repository and metadata store, and the job scheduler and project services. Failures in any layer require a different resilience approach.

Goals & Targets

GoalTarget
Job restart on worker failureAutomatic (within current job run)
Service recovery (conductor)< 5 minutes (Tier 2)
Repository RPO0 s (synchronous DB replication)
Metadata / project RPO< 60 s
Scheduled job continuityResume from last checkpoint after recovery
